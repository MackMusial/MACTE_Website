Privacy Notice

How MACTE handles the information you share through this website.

Draft for board review. Highlighted items are decisions MACTE still needs to make.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, institution (optional) and message.
  • Membership dues: when you pay through PayPal, PayPal collects your payment details. MACTE receives your name, email address, the amount paid and the date from PayPal. We never see or store card or bank account numbers.
  • This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To keep records of membership and dues payments.
  • To send association updates to member institutions' contacts. [To decide: confirm MACTE emails members, or remove this line]

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only when needed to run the association or when the law requires it.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Contact form messages are saved in a Google Sheet in MACTE's Google account and emailed to MichMACTE@gmail.com. Payment records are kept in MACTE's PayPal account. Only MACTE officers can see them.

[To decide: how long messages and payment records are kept, e.g. “We delete contact messages after two years and keep payment records for seven years.”]

Services from other companies

This site uses Google for fonts, the meeting and board listings, and some photos and documents, and PayPal for dues payments. When your browser loads these, those companies can see your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply: Google and PayPal. Links to other sites, such as AACTE, LinkedIn and Facebook, are covered by those sites' policies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete information you have sent us, email MichMACTE@gmail.com.

Children

This site is for education professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
