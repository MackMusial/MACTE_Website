Membership

Join or Renew Your MACTE Membership

Membership is open to Michigan colleges and universities with educator preparation programs, represented by their dean, chair, or director.

Why Join

Membership Benefits

Statewide Voice

Participate in shaping MACTE's advocacy on state policy affecting educator preparation programs.

Professional Network

Connect with fellow deans, chairs, and directors from institutions across Michigan.

Meetings & Events

Access to MACTE's fall and spring meetings, workshops, and the annual awards program.

AACTE Connection

Benefit from MACTE's role as Michigan's official state affiliate of AACTE.

How to Join or Renew

  1. Confirm your institution's educator preparation program is in good standing.
  2. Complete the membership form (available from the association secretary or via the Contact page).
  3. Submit annual dues payment by the renewal deadline each fall.

Renewal typically opens September 1 each year. New member institutions may join at any time.

Questions about membership?

Reach out and a MACTE officer will follow up with details on joining or renewing.
