Awards

Recognizing Excellence in Educator Preparation

Each year, MACTE honors individuals and programs that demonstrate outstanding contributions to teacher education across Michigan.

Award Categories

2026–2027 Awards

Outstanding Teacher Educator Award

Recognizes a faculty member who has made an exceptional impact on the preparation of future educators.

Distinguished Service Award

Honors sustained leadership and service to MACTE and the broader educator preparation community in Michigan.

Emerging Leader Award

Celebrates early-career faculty or administrators showing exceptional promise in teacher education leadership.

Nominations

How to Nominate

Nominations are accepted from member institutions each fall. A brief nomination form and supporting letter are required for each category.

Nominations for the 2026–2027 cycle open September 1 and close November 15. Winners are announced at the Spring Meeting.

Submit a Nomination Inquiry

Key Dates

  • Sept 1 — Nominations open
  • Nov 15 — Nominations due
  • Jan — Committee review
  • April — Awards presented at Spring Meeting
