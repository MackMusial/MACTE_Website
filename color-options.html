MACTE website · Color review

Four new color schemes for the MACTE site

The current navy and gold read as University of Michigan maize and blue. Each option below shows the real home page in a new palette. Only the colors change. The layout, fonts, and white logo stay the same.

Every option avoids the color pairs that Michigan member universities use, so the site doesn't look like it belongs to any one school.

Current A · Great Lakes Teal B · Charcoal & Cherry C · Plum & Sage D · Slate & Sea Glass
For reference

Current: Navy & Gold

Reads as U of M

What the site uses today. Deep navy with a gold accent is very close to Michigan's maize and blue. The gold text on light backgrounds is also hard to read.

Color pairs we steered away from

MACTE represents schools across the state, so the site shouldn't echo any one member's colors. These are common Michigan university pairs.

ColorsPairAssociated with
Navy and maizeUniversity of Michigan
Green and whiteMichigan State, Eastern Michigan
Green and goldWayne State, Northern Michigan
Maroon and goldCentral Michigan
Brown and goldWestern Michigan
Black and goldOakland, Michigan Tech
Red and blueSaginaw Valley State
Blue and blackGrand Valley State
