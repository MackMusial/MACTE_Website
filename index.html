About MACTE

A Statewide Network of Teacher Education Leaders

The Michigan Association of Colleges for Teacher Education (MACTE) is the state affiliate of the American Association of Colleges for Teacher Education (AACTE), the leading voice in the U.S. for educator prparation. As part of AACTE's network, MACTE promotes the learning of all PK-12 students by advancing high-quality teacher preparation and ongoing professional development for educatoring in Michigan. MACTE serves as a key platform for communication and collaboration among teacher educators, state agencies, and professional organization, advocating for shared understanding and sound policy initiatives.

Through regular meetings, shared resources, and collaborative advocacy, MACTE works to strengthen the pipeline of well-prepared educators entering Michigan classrooms.
