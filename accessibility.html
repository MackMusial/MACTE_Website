Accessibility Statement

MACTE wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use assistive technology.

Draft for board review. Highlighted items are decisions MACTE still needs to make.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we do

  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, shows where keyboard focus is, and has a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG color contrast requirements.
  • Pages use headings and landmarks in a logical order.
  • The site works on phones and when zoomed to 200%, and turns off animations and smooth scrolling for people who ask their device to reduce motion.

Known limitations

  • Some older meeting minutes and articles may not be fully accessible PDFs. If you need a document in another format, email us and we will provide one.
  • PayPal and the other sites we link to are run by other organizations, and we cannot control their accessibility.

Report a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, email MichMACTE@gmail.com or use the contact form. Tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within [To decide: number of] business days.
