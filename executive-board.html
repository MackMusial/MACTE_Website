Committees

Standing Committees

Advocacy & Policy Committee

Monitors state and federal policy affecting educator preparation and represents MACTE's interests to the Michigan Department of Education.

Awards Committee

Coordinates the annual awards program, including nominations, review, and recognition of outstanding teacher educators.

Membership & Outreach Committee

Supports member engagement, recruits new member institutions, and coordinates communication across the association.

Program Committee

Plans the agenda and content for MACTE's fall and spring meetings.
